Bar: Bellocq Tea Atelier live homepage.

## Run completed

The production page is live and returns HTTP 200. The reference bar was re-fetched from Bellocq's live homepage after deployment.

Harsh comparison areas:
- Hero: Tecito now has a six-scene CDMX scroll narrative instead of a conventional campaign banner.
- Story: Tecito communicates the House of Editions model, finite seasonal drops, truth-tag ritual, and commissioned editions in one path.
- Motion: four Seedance 2.5 clips are used; two people-dependent transitions remain intentional still scenes because the video provider rejected those requests under partner validation.
- Restraint: motion is limited to the cinematic opening and small interaction feedback; reduced-motion behavior is present.
- Conversion: Tecito has a single first-edition waitlist path plus the commissioned-editions path.

## Critic result

The biggest remaining gap versus the Bellocq bar is not concept or hierarchy; it is production proof and material authenticity. Bellocq has mature real-world product photography, merchandise depth, and established retail trust signals. Tecito's MVP uses generated campaign media and does not yet have final physical product photography or a live product checkout.

That gap cannot be truthfully 'fixed' with invented merchandise. The correct next gauntlet round begins after the first physical sample/packaging photography and real sellable SKU exist.
