# TECITO Visual Gauntlet

Bar: Bellocq Tea Atelier live homepage, compared at matched desktop and mobile viewports.

Judge these independently: hero, typography, color/material feel, motion restraint, edition storytelling, mobile composition, and CTA clarity.

Rules:
- The critic is separate from the builder.
- The critic compares actual rendered output against the live reference, not a written description.
- The judgment is binary: ours or reference.
- Name the single biggest gap, fix it, and repeat.
- Do not stop at a score or after a fixed number of rounds.
